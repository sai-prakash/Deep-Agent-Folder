Origin & attribution

Inspired by a public skills map.
Built independently.

Andrew Ng and DeepLearning.AI published the AI Engineering Skills Map in August 2026 after synthesizing job-posting, interview, survey and online evidence. It identifies four high-level competencies for modern AI engineering.

AI Engineering Mastery is an independent open-source learning product that turns those ideas—and additional current engineering research—into executable practice, diagnostics and evidence gates.

Andrew Ng and DeepLearning.AI have not sponsored, endorsed, reviewed or certified this product. Their names and source are used only for factual attribution.
