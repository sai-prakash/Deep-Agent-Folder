Why this exists

AI engineering is not a framework checklist.

The market moves too fast for “finish LangChain, then learn agents” roadmaps. Durable capability comes from understanding mechanisms, observing failures, running evals and making engineering decisions under real constraints.

01

Build

Implement the smallest working system. Inspect intermediate state instead of trusting the final answer.

02

Break + measure

Trigger a meaningful failure on purpose. Trace where reality first diverges from your expectation.

03

Defend

Improve one mechanism, rerun the same eval, check regressions, then defend why this architecture is worth its cost and risk.
