## LinkedIn — Security post

Your agent completed the task.

It also leaked a secret because a retrieved webpage told it to.

Pass or fail?

This is why “the demo works” is a terrible definition of AI engineering mastery.

A useful agent is also a new attack surface:

retrieved content → prompt injection → tool permissions → credentials → network egress → irreversible action.

The correct engineering question is not only:

> Can the model do the task?

It is:

> What is the blast radius when the model, context or tool output is wrong or adversarial?

I included this scenario in the free AI Engineering diagnostic I just launched.

12 questions. No signup. About 5 minutes.

## LinkedIn — Why free/open core

Why am I keeping the core AI Engineering course free?

Because information is not the scarce thing anymore.

There are excellent free courses, documentation, papers and models everywhere.

The scarce thing is knowing whether you can actually:

- localize a failure
- design an eval
- interpret a trace
- choose the simpler architecture
- detect a regression
- protect an agent from unsafe actions
- defend a system under cost/latency/security constraints

So AI Engineering Mastery is open-core.

The free layer teaches and lets you practice.
