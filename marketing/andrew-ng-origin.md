# Andrew Ng / AI Engineering Skills Map — attribution & marketing playbook

## The rule

Use Andrew Ng’s name **only as factual attribution to the public source that inspired the project**. Never use his name, photo, likeness, logo, DeepLearning.AI branding, or course imagery in a way that suggests sponsorship, partnership, endorsement, review, certification, or official affiliation.

Recommended disclosure:

> Inspired by Andrew Ng & DeepLearning.AI’s public 2026 AI Engineering Skills Map. AI Engineering Mastery is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, reviewed by, or certified by Andrew Ng or DeepLearning.AI.

Source:
- Andrew Ng writing: https://www.andrewng.org/writing
- Original Aug. 14, 2026 post: https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2088302050706686198

## How we leverage it

### 1. Origin story — strongest use

> Andrew Ng published a useful map of the skills modern AI engineers need. I wanted to answer the next question: **how do you actually prove you have them?**
>
> So I turned that inspiration into an open mastery lab: executable systems, deliberate failures, evals, traces and evidence gates.

This makes Andrew’s work the *starting point*, not our product claim.

### 2. “Map → practice” content series

Four posts, each clearly framed as our interpretation/implementation:

1. **Build & deploy AI systems →** show an executable lab + eval.
2. **Software engineering fundamentals →** show a dependency/reproducibility failure.
3. **Using coding agents →** show context/delegation/verification workflow.
4. **Shaping the build →** show a system-design decision where the right answer is *not* more agents.

Close each post with:

> Source inspiration: Andrew Ng & DeepLearning.AI’s 2026 AI Engineering Skills Map. This implementation is independent and unaffiliated.

### 3. Diagnostic hook

Safe:

> Andrew Ng’s 2026 skills map is a useful answer to “what should AI engineers know?” I built a free diagnostic around a harder question: **what do you do when the system fails?**

Avoid:
- “Andrew Ng AI Engineering Diagnostic”
- “Andrew Ng inspired certificate”
- “Based on Andrew Ng curriculum”
- “Andrew Ng course, but practical”
- “Official implementation of the AI Engineering Skills Map”

### 4. Launch-post version

> Andrew Ng and DeepLearning.AI recently published an evidence-backed AI Engineering Skills Map after analyzing 10,000+ job postings plus interviews, surveys and other data.
>
> I loved the direction—but a map still leaves one hard question: **How do you know you can actually do the work?**
>
> I built AI Engineering Mastery as an independent answer: 101 lessons, 76 executable labs, deliberate failure analysis, evals and evidence-gated mastery.
>
> The first thing I’m launching is free: a 12-scenario AI Engineering diagnostic.
>
> [product link]
>
> Source inspiration: Andrew Ng & DeepLearning.AI. Independent project; no affiliation or endorsement.

### 5. Outreach

After we have real usage evidence—not before—share the project with Andrew Ng / DeepLearning.AI as a respectful community implementation:

> Your AI Engineering Skills Map pushed me to build an open, evidence-driven mastery lab that turns the ideas into executable practice. It’s independent and clearly attributed. We’ve now seen [real usage/outcome evidence]. Sharing in case it’s useful to your team/community.

Do not ask for endorsement in the first message. Earn the right to be noticed through product quality and learner evidence.

## Why this is the best trade-off

The name creates a strong, timely origin hook because the map is current and evidence-backed. The commercial asset, however, must remain **our transformation layer**: diagnostic → weakest-skill route → executable mastery → evidence → benchmark/portfolio proof.

Andrew Ng is the source inspiration. **Evidence-driven mastery is the brand.**
