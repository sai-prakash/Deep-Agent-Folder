# AI Engineering Mastery — Founding Launch Operating Plan

## Product thesis

**Category:** evidence-driven AI engineering mastery / apprenticeship system.

**Positioning:**

> Stop learning AI. Prove you can engineer it.

The product is not differentiated by having more AI content. Free alternatives already provide strong agent courses and certificates. The differentiator is the **evidence loop**: build → break → trace → measure → improve → defend → verify.

## Market evidence used for this launch

- Hyperskill's 2026 Agentic AI Engineering cohort is listed at $2,499 and explicitly targets experienced engineers building reliable production-grade LLM systems: https://maven.com/hyperskill/ai-engineer-bootcamp
- Hamel Husain + Shreya Shankar's AI Evals course is listed at $4,200 with hundreds of reviews, validating willingness to pay for evaluation/reliability depth: https://maven.com/parlance-labs/evals
- Product Faculty's Agentic Engineering Certificate is listed at $2,500 and the Aug–Sep 2026 cohort is sold out: https://maven.com/product-faculty/frontier-agentic-engineering-certificate
- Hugging Face provides a strong Agents Course and certification for free, so charging only for access to information is a weak moat: https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/en/unit0/introduction
- Current developer discussions repeatedly express frustration with shallow/outdated framework courses and ask for project-driven, production-grade learning. Example: https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions/comments/1sp12a6/trying_to_upskill_and_ai_engineer_courses_feel/

## Open-core decision

V2 is already released under MIT. Keep it free and public. Do not attempt to sell access to the existing repository as the primary product.

Free/open core becomes:
- trust engine
- GitHub discovery
- product demo
- learning utility
- lead generation

Paid value should come from:
- guided mastery
- deeper diagnostics
- review/evaluation
- benchmarks
- cohort accountability
- human feedback
- portfolio-grade evidence
- hosted progress / adaptive learning later

## Founding offer

### Phase 0 — free founding beta

Goal: recruit **20 qualified engineers**, not maximize signups.

Candidate profile:
- software / frontend / design / data / ML engineer or technical PM
- can code independently
- has used LLM APIs or coding agents
- wants production AI engineering skill, not prompt tricks
- can commit at least 4 focused hours/week

Beta experience hypothesis:
- baseline diagnostic
- recommended mastery path
- one meaningful build challenge/week
- failure/eval evidence required
- capstone artifact
- direct product feedback

No money is collected by the initial landing page. The first objective is proof of activation and outcome.

### Phase 1 — paid Founding Mastery Sprint

Only after the free beta produces evidence.

Price hypotheses to test, not promises:
- India: ₹14,999–₹24,999
- Global: $299–$499

Do not choose the final price by intuition. Interview beta users and test willingness-to-pay at multiple price points.

### Phase 2 — Pro software

Hypothesis:
- India: ₹999–₹1,999/month
- Global: $19–$39/month

Paid software must deliver something the public repository cannot:
- identity + cloud progress
- skill graph
- adaptive next task
- hosted evaluations/traces
- model comparisons
- private projects
- benchmark history
- portfolio verification

## Launch success criteria

The first launch is successful if it creates evidence, not vanity traffic.

### Activation metrics

Track manually during founding beta:
- diagnostic starts
- diagnostic completions
- founding applications
- accepted beta users
- users who complete first recommended lab
- week-1 active users
- users who submit reproducible evidence
- capstone completion

### Decision thresholds

Before paid acquisition, aim to observe at least:
- 100 qualified diagnostic completions
- 20 credible founding applications
- 10 active beta participants
- 5 participants completing meaningful evidence-backed work
- 3 users who explicitly say they would pay for the guided product

These are decision thresholds, not universal benchmark rates.

## Paid ads policy

**Do not run broad paid ads before organic activation is proven.**

Paid traffic cannot repair weak positioning, broken activation, or absent social proof.

When organic evidence exists, test in this order:
1. Reddit ads → diagnostic, not checkout
2. Google Search → high-intent terms such as AI engineering course, agent engineering course, AI eval course
3. LinkedIn retargeting → cohort/pro offer, not broad cold acquisition

Kill ads quickly if qualified diagnostic completion / application economics are poor.

## Organic launch campaign

### Launch post 1 — origin

Hook:

> I got tired of AI courses where “finished the lesson” counts as learning. So I built one where it doesn't.

Show:
- lesson
- executable lab
- deliberate break
- measured result
- mastery gate

CTA: free diagnostic.

### Launch post 2 — diagnostic

Hook:

> You can build a RAG demo. Can you diagnose why it is wrong?

Show a screenshot of the score card and weakest-skill result.

CTA: test your level.

### Launch post 3 — eval wedge

Hook:

> Ten examples looked better after the prompt change. Would you ship?

Use one diagnostic scenario, then explain the production answer.

CTA: diagnostic.

### Launch post 4 — agent failure

Hook:

> Your agent completed the task. It also leaked a secret. Pass or fail?

CTA: production/security skill diagnostic.

### Reddit / HN framing

Do not sell first.

Use:

> I built a free AI engineering mastery lab where passing requires reproducible implementation evidence, failure analysis and measurement. I also added a short diagnostic. I want experienced engineers to break both the product and the assumptions behind it.

## Product roadmap after beta evidence

Do not expand lesson count merely to signal depth.

Highest-value V3 product work:
1. persistent skill graph
2. deeper coding-agent / harness engineering track
3. context engineering + skills
4. current MCP / A2A labs
5. eval arena with repeated trials
6. long-running/sandbox agent challenges
7. security arena
8. shaping-the-build scenarios
9. portfolio evidence pages
10. verified mastery / human review workflow

## Commercial guardrails

- Existing V2 code/content is MIT licensed; do not represent it as exclusive paid IP.
- Future proprietary hosted features or paid curriculum should have deliberate licensing from inception.
- Do not use employer/customer/confidential material in public examples, beta submissions or marketing.
- Do not promise job outcomes or market the directional diagnostic as a validated hiring assessment.
